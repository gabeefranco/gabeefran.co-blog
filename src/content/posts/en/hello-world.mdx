I finally sat down and built this thing. Not because the world needed another blog, but because I kept having half-formed thoughts about code, systems, and the history of the tools I use every day — and nowhere to put them.

So: hello. I'm Gabe. This is going to be a small, mostly technical corner of the internet where I write things down so I understand them better, and maybe so future-me (or you) can come back to them later.

## What to expect

A few recurring themes, roughly in order of how often I think about them:

- **How things work under the hood** — CPUs, compilers, operating systems, the kind of stuff that's easy to use and hard to explain.
- **Open source history** — the people and decisions that shaped the software we take for granted.
- **Whatever I'm learning at PUCRS** that seems worth writing about properly instead of just leaving in a notebook.

I write in English and Portuguese, since both are useful to me and hopefully to whoever reads this. Every post has both versions — use the language switch in the header to move between them.

No comment section, no newsletter, no tracking scripts I didn't write myself. Just posts. Let's see where this goes.
